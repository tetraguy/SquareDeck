# Changelog

All notable changes to SquareDeck, newest first. The launcher's update page reads this file and shows every
version newer than the one installed (each section starts at its "## version" heading).

## 8.1.25
- Twitch on the Multimedia page's Streaming tab now shows the Twitch logo on its tile and in the menu, instead of its name on purple.

## 8.1.24
- The Switch 2 style Settings (formerly New Settings UI in Experimental) is now the default look for Settings, turned on for everyone with this update.
- Its switch now lives in Settings > Display, under "Settings view and unlockables". Turn it off to go back to the classic Settings and its five layouts (Settings view style only shows while it's off).

## 8.1.23
- New Settings UI (Experimental) now follows your UI style: the page, text, lines, highlight card, switches, highlight ring and glow take the style's own colors (and its gradient ring when it has one), and the style's font carries through. The Switch style keeps the exact Basic White / Basic Black look.
- Choosing a UI style in Themes shows on the settings page straight away, no reopen needed.

## 8.1.22

- Experimental: **New Settings UI** (Looks and sound). Settings drawn like the Nintendo Switch 2's System Settings:
  - A plain light (Basic White) or dark (Basic Black) page, following your theme, with "System Settings" and the gear at the top, a line under it, and a line over the button hints with the handheld at the left.
  - The sections listed on the left as plain names, ruled into groups, the one you're in in blue with a bar beside it, and a thin line between the list and the settings.
  - Settings as flat rows on thin lines, no icons, values in blue, pill switches (blue when on), and section titles with a short bar.
  - The highlight as a white card with a soft blue, purple and pink rim.
  - Choice pop-ups as a floating card with a round mark beside each choice, filled in blue for the one in use.
  Settings reopens in the new look when you switch it on or off.

## 8.1.21

- Retro Lounge's experimental features are out of Experimental and always there now (all but Surprise me, which stays in Experimental):
  - Shaders and bezels per console, and box art and screenshots from ScreenScraper: Retro Lounge > Setup > Looks and artwork.
  - The save state browser: on each RetroArch game's page, with "Load" to start straight from a save.

## 8.1.20

## 8.1.0

Eighteen new experimental features for the Game Bar widget (widget 1.6), each with its own switch in Settings >
Experimental > Game Bar widget. Rebuild the widget package in Visual Studio to get them.

- **In the Power card**
  - **Frame cap buttons:** Off, 30, 40 and 60 FPS, applied straight away with Radeon's limiter, and Save to keep
    that cap for the game.
  - **Auto TDP:** pick 30, 40 or 60 FPS and SquareDeck finds the lowest TDP that holds it, a watt or two at a time
    every few seconds. Needs TDP control and the frame counter (PresentMon).
- **Thermal warning:** the Live card turns amber, with a note, within 5 degrees of the chip's temperature limit.
- **New cards**
  - **Game notes:** the running game's notes, and Add note (saved with the date) for codes, hints and where you
    left off.
  - **Achievements:** progress for the running Steam game, the next locked ones, and How to get this for each.
  - **Ask Genie:** type a question or pick one, and Game Genie answers about the game you're playing.
  - **Controller:** controller battery, rumble strength, and a stick drift check.
  - **Friends online:** Steam friends who are online or playing, with Message.
  - **Notifications:** SquareDeck's latest notifications.
  - **Downloads:** what Steam and SquareDeck are downloading, Steam's downloads page, and stopping SquareDeck's queue.
  - **Launch:** your apps (Discord, a browser, Spotify…) and SquareDeck shortcuts (Home, Library, Retro Lounge,
    Captures, Settings).
- **Invite** on the game card: Steam's friends list for Steam games; for others it points to Game Bar's Xbox Social.
- **Card order and hiding:** an Edit button in the header to move cards up and down and hide the ones you don't use.
- **Layout per game:** each game remembers the card order, hidden cards and Live tiles from the last time.
- **Presets per game:** Save this setup to the game (cards, Live tiles and Quick tiles), in the card editor.
- **Match my style:** the widget takes on the current SquareDeck style's colors, font and wallpaper, Mythical
  styles included.
- **Companion:** your pixel companion in the header, cheering achievements, worrying about low battery and
  suggesting a break after long sessions.

## 8.0.0

Twenty-four new experimental features. Each is off until you turn it on in Settings > Experimental, where most also
have a page of their own options.

- **While you play**
  - **Frame rate cap per game:** 30, 36, 40, 45, 50, 60, 72, 90 or 120 FPS in a game's profile, or one cap for
    every game. Applied when the game starts and put back when it closes, with Radeon's own limiter (Frame Rate
    Target Control, or Radeon Chill where that isn't offered).
  - **Docked / TV mode:** when a TV or monitor is connected, text and controls grow to 130%, the TDP goes to its
    highest, the mouse pointer hides and the sound moves to the TV. Unplug and it all goes back.
  - **Session timer and break reminder:** a nudge over the game after the time you choose, and once when the
    battery gets low.
  - **Launch options per game:** command-line options for any game (Steam ones through Steam's run link), and for
    games started from their own program, environment variables and "Run as administrator".
  - **Scripts before and after a game:** a program, script or command run hidden before it starts (waiting for it,
    if you like) and after it closes.
  - **Smooth return after playing:** Home fades back in on the game you launched.
  - **Charger check:** says when you're plugged in but the battery is still going down.
- **Library and Home**
  - **Smart collections:** Library chips that fill themselves: Never played, Played once and left, Short sessions,
    Best on battery, Not played in a year, Big and rarely played, and Most played.
  - **Daily pick:** once a day, a game you haven't played in a while and why it's worth another look.
  - **Game Pass shelf:** a Game Pass button in the Library with Xbox's "Recently added" and "Leaving soon" lists,
    the month's announcements, and your Xbox games.
  - **Trailer previews on Home:** rest on a Steam game and its trailer plays, muted, behind the home row.
  - **microSD awareness:** a Library chip for each card, with its games still listed when it's out, and a notice
    when a card goes in or comes out.
- **Retro Lounge**
  - **Box art and screenshots from ScreenScraper:** fills in missing covers and screenshots (free account and a
    ScreenScraper developer ID needed).
  - **Shaders and bezels per console:** a shader and a bezel for each console's RetroArch games, with suggested
    CRT and LCD looks.
  - **Save state browser:** a game's save states with their pictures; pick one to start the game from it.
  - **Surprise me:** a random game, to look at or to play straight away.
- **Friends and media**
  - **Friends playing on Home:** a small card with the Steam friends in a game right now.
  - **Achievement pop-ups:** a pop-up in your style's colors over the game when you unlock a Steam achievement or a
    RetroAchievement.
  - **Twitch:** on the Multimedia page's Streaming tab.
- **System and troubleshooting**
  - **Crash recovery:** after an unexpected close, an offer to go back to the page you were on, or to send the log.
  - **Controller tester:** every button, trigger and stick live, a drift check, and a rumble test.
  - **Windows Update guard:** warns during a game when Windows wants to restart, and sets active hours or pauses
    updates for a week.
  - **Driver check:** graphics, BIOS and ASUS driver versions and dates, flagging any over six months old, with
    links to the official downloads.
  - **Startup apps manager:** turn apps that start with Windows on or off, right in SquareDeck.

## 7.3.45

- **Device health** (Settings home, and the top of your device's section):
  - Who's in control of the lights, the TDP and the buttons right now: SquareDeck, Armoury Crate SE, Windows' Dynamic
    Lighting, or nobody.
  - Checks for what gets in the way, most serious first, each with a one-press fix:
    - Lights: ASUS Optimization off, Dynamic Lighting on, "Awake" off, lights at 5%, the light controller not found.
    - Hardware and conflicts: the controller settings device not found, ASUS's firmware driver missing, Armoury Crate
      SE running alongside, ASUS Optimization putting ASUS's power limits back.
    - TDP: the TDP can't be set, the helper is out of date, the last change failed.
    - Widget, battery and storage: the widget missing or old, battery wear, low disk space.
  - Any check can be set aside with "Don't warn me about this".
  - A little after start, a notice appears if something needs fixing; it can be turned off.
- **Collect a report** (on Device health): one zip in Downloads with SquareDeck's logs, the version, the device, the
  health checks and the settings. API keys, tokens and passwords are left out.
- **Settings:**
  - Each section's group cards show how many settings you've changed from the default.
  - "Reset ‹section›" at the bottom puts them back.
  - For a minute after a reset or a new theme, Undo sits at the top of Settings.
  - The old ROG Ally controls page now sends you to the one place for those settings, in your device's section.
- **Game profiles cover more:**
  - Fan curve, rumble strength, the Xbox button light, and the Game Bar widget's own quick tiles while the game runs.
  - Suggested settings: Battery saver, Balanced or Performance, sized to your processor's TDP range, with one
    suggested from how the game actually ran.
- **Battery and performance by game:**
  - While a game runs, SquareDeck notes the battery, TDP, temperature, refresh rate and frame rate. The frame rate
    needs the overlay's frame counter.
  - Each game shows how long a full battery lasts ("about 1 h 50 m at 15 W"), with its sessions.
  - Best battery for this game: a quarter less power than it has been using, at the lower refresh rate, with Undo.
  - See it in Settings > Library and stores, your device's section, or the game's profile.
- **Retro Lounge:**
  - Each game page lists the controls in its emulator. RetroArch gets its menu on L3 + R3 and Quit on
    LB + RB + Start + Back, unless you set your own.
  - RetroArch save states and screenshots show on the game's page.
  - RetroAchievements: sign in with your web API key (Setup) and see each game's achievements and points. Cartridge
    games and their .zip files are matched. It can also turn achievements on in RetroArch for your account.
  - Check ROMs against No-Intro or Redump DAT files (Setup): verified copies, ones not in your DAT files, and
    duplicates. The verdict also shows on each game's page.
- **First-run setup:** on a handheld, it now asks who should run the power, lights and buttons: SquareDeck or the
  maker's app (Armoury Crate SE on the Ally), so the two don't fight.
- **Polish:**
  - Undo after a theme change.
  - What's new shows once after an update; it can be turned off, and the current version's notes are in Settings >
    Updates and data. The changelog now comes with SquareDeck.
  - Button names can match the controller: PlayStation or Switch names when one is connected, Xbox otherwise. This
    is the default for new installs.
  - Settings rows are named for screen readers.
  - Windows' high contrast thickens the highlight, and Windows' "Animation effects" off reduces motion here too.

- **Every UI style except Switch.** The other 82 styles are gone while theming is rebuilt from the ground up.
  Settings > Look and feel no longer has a UI style choice, and first-time setup asks only for light or dark.
  - If you were using another style, SquareDeck moves you to Switch the first time 6.4.0 starts. When your style had
    brought its own colors, wallpaper, sounds, music, moving background and button pictures, those go back to
    Switch's as well; if you'd turned that off, your own choices are kept.
  - Your choices of button style, button shelf, footer, game art, font and color theme all still work; their
    "From the UI style" option is now called "Default".
  - Themes you saved still restore their colors, wallpaper, sounds and music, on Switch.
  - The moving backgrounds, wallpapers, music and sound packs the styles used are all still there to pick yourself.

## 6.3.0

### New
- **How long to beat on game pages.** Each game's page shows how long it takes to finish (main story, main + extras,
  completionist) from HowLongToBeat.com, with your own play time beside it ("about 65% of the main story"). Press the
  card to open the game's HowLongToBeat page. Each game is looked up once and kept on this PC (hltb.json), so the
  page shows it straight away next time; it's checked again after a month, or after a few days when the game wasn't
  found. Turn it off, or forget the kept times, in Settings > Library and stores.
- **Web links open in SquareDeck's browser.** Links from news, game pages, the update page, settings and everywhere
  else now open in the built-in browser instead of Windows' default browser, bringing SquareDeck to the front. Turn it
  off in Settings > Network and browser > "Open web links in SquareDeck". The browser's own menu can still send a page
  to Windows' browser.
- **Five new splash animations** while a game starts: Spotlight (light rays turn behind the cover as it zooms in),
  Flip (the cover turns like a card, then shines), Ripples, Loading ring and Cinematic (letterbox bars and a gliding
  cover). The original is still there as Classic, and Surprise me picks one each time. Settings > Gaming > Splash
  animation shows each one as you pick it.
- **Five new Performance Boost animations,** each with its own vibration: Supernova, Radar, Helix, Data rain and
  Battery charge. That's fifteen in all.
- **Punch-in when opening a game or app.** The tile is pressed in, punches forward and settles as it opens (home
  screen, library, apps and game pages). Switch it off in Settings > Home screen > Animations.
- **My Device, redesigned.** A dashboard: the device and what's inside it as chips beside a battery ring at the top,
  round live gauges for processor, graphics, temperature and fan, then Power cards (power draw with a graph, battery
  health, battery pace, power limit, controller battery, brightness) and System cards (memory, every drive with how full
  it is, display with refresh rate and scale, network with name, signal, address and speed, sound, Windows version and
  build, time on). Every card can be pressed to open the settings it belongs to.
- **Captures: delete or export albums.** On the Albums view, press {Details} on an album for Export as ZIP or Delete
  album; inside an album the same two buttons sit beside the filters. Exports go to your Downloads folder as one ZIP of
  every capture in the album; deleting moves them all to the Recycle Bin.

### Changed
- **The update page shows the changelog.** What's new now comes from
  [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/tetraguy/SquareDeck/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md), with every version since the one you
  have, instead of the release's description. There's also a button to open the changelog.
- **Steam games' artwork comes from Steam.** Banners behind the home screen, logos, tall covers and headers for Steam
  games are now Steam's own; SteamGridDB is used when Steam has none, and for square covers and icons (which Steam
  doesn't make). The first time 6.3.0 starts, the copies downloaded from SteamGridDB before are let go so Steam's are
  fetched instead; pictures you picked yourself stay.
- **New custom games icon** in the library (a game cartridge on green), with a matching pixel-art version.

### Fixed
- **The library highlight grows with the cover.** With a growing highlight animation, only the cover grew and the ring
  around it stayed the same size. The whole tile grows now, ring included.
- **The brightness slider is there as soon as the quick settings open.** It used to appear a moment later, because the
  level was read by starting PowerShell each time. It's now read straight from Windows (in a few milliseconds),
  read once when SquareDeck starts, and the last level is shown at once and corrected after. Setting it is faster too.
- **Black flashes with a moving background.** Two causes: the video was looped by jumping back to the start by hand,
  which showed a black frame on some PCs each time it came round (it now loops with a repeating media clock), and the
  copy of the video behind pages re-measured itself whenever the video had no picture for a moment, flashing black
  or jumping (it's now fixed to the background's own size). A moving background that's already blurred also isn't
  blurred a second time behind each page in glass styles.
- **The SteamGridDB key is no longer sent to image servers.** Picture downloads went out with the SteamGridDB API key
  attached, including to Steam's and SteamGridDB's image servers, which don't need it. Pictures are now downloaded
  without it; only SteamGridDB's API gets the key.

## 6.1.3

### Changed
- **A quicker, smoother interface.** Several things were slowing it down, most of them on handhelds:
  - **Controller polling.** SquareDeck asked all four controller slots for their state sixty times a second, and
    asking an empty slot is slow in Windows. Empty slots are now looked at once a second, and a controller plugged in
    is still found within a second.
  - **Holding a direction** repeats sooner (after 0.32 s instead of 0.4 s), steps faster, and speeds up again after
    a few steps, so long rows and lists are quicker to get through.
  - **Navigation sounds** start on a thread of their own. Starting a sound could hold up the interface for a moment
    on every move of the highlight.
  - **Blurred game artwork behind the home screen and pages** is blurred once when it loads. Before, the graphics card
    redid a full-screen blur whenever anything in front of it moved, and every page opened with that blur being worked
    out. Styles with only a slight blur keep the live one.
  - **Scrolling the game row** is timed by the clock rather than by frames, so it's just as quick with Performance
    mode's 30 frames a second, and settles in about a sixth of a second.
  - **Pages open faster.** Their opening animations are a quarter shorter, and while one moves in the page is drawn
    once and moved as a picture, rather than every part of it being redrawn each frame.
  - **Settings** shows a category's settings sooner when the highlight rests on it.
  - **Less repeated work while a game runs.** The running-game banner, its icon and the home screen's button hints are
    only redone when something changed, not every second. Checks for how the power limit is set (asked several times
    a second) are worked out every few seconds instead of each time.

  - **Startup:** "Open Xbox mode when Windows starts" boots straight into SquareDeck.

  - **Needs:** Windows 11 24H2 or later, an NTFS drive, and Developer Mode while registering, because the capability
    belongs to Microsoft. AnyFSE and other non-Microsoft home apps need the same. If SquareDeck's folder moves, it
    registers again from the new place when it starts.

  colors, type, corners, the shape of the buttons, tile size, the game art behind it, the focus glow and pop-ups.
  The picker shows a small drawing of each style.
  - **Switch**: the original look.
  - **Xbox**: full-screen game art fading into the background, a row of smaller tiles where the selected one
    grows with its name across the bottom, buttons as labelled cards, and the Xbox green highlight.
  - **PlayStation 5**: small tiles across the top, the selected one much bigger, the game's title large at the
    lower left over its art, a "Play Game" button, and a white highlight.
  - **Steam Big Picture**: art across the screen, a "Recent games" label over the row, the game's name and details
    under it, the buttons as tabs, a dark hint bar along the bottom, and the Steam blue highlight.
  - **Neon** (new): near-black with glowing magenta edges, a faint cyan grid over everything, sharp corners and
    Bahnschrift type.
  - **Minimal** (new): flat with no shadows, small round buttons, more space between tiles, and one warm orange
    accent. It follows the light or dark theme.
  - **Retro** (new): an old amber monitor, with scan lines, chunky square edges and Consolas type.
  - **Glass**: frosted, see-through panels and buttons with bright edges over heavily blurred, colorful art. It
    uses the game's art, your wallpaper, or a built-in gradient.

  Choosing Xbox, PlayStation 5, Steam Big Picture or Glass turns on the artwork behind the home screen. A highlight
  color you picked yourself is kept. High-contrast themes still win over any style.
- **Pages show the game's art** in the Xbox, PlayStation, Steam, Neon and Glass styles: blurred and dimmed behind
  every page, the way the consoles do it.

### Changed
- **Explorer**, rebuilt along the lines of Handheld Explorer:
  - A sidebar with Quick access, Pinned folders and Drives, each drive with a bar showing how full it is.
  - A top bar with sidebar, back, up, a clickable breadcrumb path, filter, grid/list and menu.
  - Big tiles with picture thumbnails, or a list with date and size.
  - Tick several items with Y, then copy, cut, paste, delete, zip or check their properties together.
  - LB / RB go back and forward. LT / RT page. View switches grid and list. Menu opens the main menu.
  - New folder, natural sorting (by name, date, size or type), hidden files, pin folders, copy the path,
    show in Windows Explorer.
  - Updates when files change. Press and hold (touch) or right-click for actions.
  - The view, sort, hidden files and pins are remembered.
- **Task Manager**, rebuilt along the lines of Handheld Task Manager:
  - **Apps** shows programs with a window. **Processes** shows everything, grouped by program. **Performance**
    has CPU and memory with a minute of history, battery, network speed, storage and system.
  - CPU, RAM and battery are always in the top bar.
  - Actions: switch to, close (letting the app save), end task, priority, open file location, details.
  - X ends a task (asks first), Y changes the sort, LB / RB switch tabs, and the update speed can be changed.
  - The order holds still while you move through the list. Windows system processes and SquareDeck itself can't
    be ended from it.
- **News**: LB / RB move between Latest, Patches and updates, Announcements and Sales. Y still moves to the next one.
